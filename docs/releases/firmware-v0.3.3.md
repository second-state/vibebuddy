## What's new

- The first firmware released on its own. Vibe Buddy 0.3.3 and later download it and offer it on the Device tab.
- Characters: the buddy wears the look, voice and lines of the Character you pick, with lines for more moments: the first done of the day, milestones, late nights, a morning greeting, long sessions and coming back after a break.
- A menu on the box: long-press K1, then step through with short presses to change the volume, mute, end a Pomodoro phase or see the box's status.
- The box reports its firmware version, so the app can tell which firmware is newer.
