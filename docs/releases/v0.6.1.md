## What's new

- 0.6.1 fixes the update from 0.5.0, which stopped with "An error occurred while running the updater".
- A new look for the App on Mac and Linux, with the box in the sidebar.
- A new look for the box: the korekore screens, or the robot screens as before.
- Voice commands (Beta): hold K0 on the box and ask your Codex or Claude Code. It works over the cable, Wi-Fi and the Relay, and uses your own keys: Groq to hear you, and ElevenLabs if you want the reply spoken in the Character's voice.
- Make your own Character: write its lines and pick a voice.
- Updates over Wi-Fi and the Relay: firmware and Character packs reach the box with no cable, carry on if the link drops, and go back to the old firmware if the new one fails.
- Links off the cable are encrypted end to end.
- Boxes with a touchscreen: tap the menu, tap a task card to open its agent, and pet the buddy.
- Task cards show each agent's icon, fill two pages, and come only from the computer the box is linked to.
- An optional VibeBuddy account. The box, the agents and your Characters work without one.
- Also: the box is found on the local network by name, a more stable link over the cable, clearer Wi-Fi errors, and many small fixes.

## Before you update

- Needs box firmware 0.6.0, offered in Settings → Device. From 0.5.0, this update needs the cable once.
- Update VibeBuddy on every computer paired with the box: after the box has firmware 0.6.0, VibeBuddy 0.5.0 reaches it only over the cable.
- Linux (AUR): run `systemctl --user restart vibebuddyd` after the update. `install.sh` does this for you.
