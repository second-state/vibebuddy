## What's new

- Characters: pick who the buddy is on the new Character tab. Amanda, Jackson, Wanwan Xiaohe and Ahu each have their own look, voice and lines; you can choose what the buddy calls you, or make your own character from a few drawings.
- Updates: Vibe Buddy now checks for new versions once a day (Settings → General). Firmware is released on its own and downloaded for you; the Device tab offers it. From this version on, the Mac app updates itself through its own dialog. See "Updates" in the README for exactly what is sent.
- Update the box's firmware from the Device tab after installing: Characters need firmware 0.3.3.
- The box gets its own menu (long-press K1), which now sets the volume; the volume slider is gone from the app. The Device tab shows the box's screen at full size.
- A Muse devkit or another ESP32-S3 plugged in next to the box is left alone.
