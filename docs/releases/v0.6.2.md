## What's new

- VibeBuddy for Windows 10 and 11, with a tray icon. The installer isn't signed yet: when SmartScreen warns, choose More info, then Run anyway.
- Holiday decorations on the box: Thanksgiving and Christmas, over either set of screens. Two holiday Characters come with them: Tom and Pip.
- The box remembers up to 5 Wi-Fi networks and joins the one in range, so it can move between office and home without the cable.
- Also: pick the box's Wi-Fi from the networks it hears, your own Character shows as a card on the Character page, the App says which computer has the box when another one has it, and small fixes.

## Before you update

- Decorations and several Wi-Fi networks need box firmware 0.6.1, offered in Settings → Device.
- Linux (AUR): run `systemctl --user restart vibebuddyd` after the update. `install.sh` does this for you.
