## What's new

- Updates over Wi-Fi and the Relay. After this one, firmware and Character packs reach the box over any link, carry on where they stopped if the link drops, and go back to the previous firmware if a new one fails to start. The screen shows the progress; hold K2 to cancel.
- Links off the cable are encrypted end to end. The box keeps its key and the computers paired with it.
- A new look to choose in the App: the korekore screens, or the robot screens as before.
- Boxes with a touchscreen: tap the menu, tap a task card to open its agent, and pet the box.
- Task cards show each agent's icon and fill two pages.
- Voice commands keep your first words: the box listens from the moment K0 goes down.
- Also: the box is found on the local network by name, says in words why it couldn't join Wi-Fi, and keeps its speaker quiet while busy.
- From 0.5.0, this update needs the cable once.
- Works with VibeBuddy 0.6.0 and later. Update the App on every computer paired with the box first: an older App can't reach this firmware over Wi-Fi or the Relay.
