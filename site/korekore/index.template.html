HOW IT WORKS

Quiet while they work. Loud only when it matters.

Every agent session becomes a card on the box, newest on top. Colour always means state, and Kore only speaks when you are needed.

{{kore:working:6}}
WORKING

Keep coding.

A calm blue screen and no sound. The card shows which agent, which session and for how long.

{{kore:input:6}}
NEEDS YOU

Kore kore!

The whole screen turns yellow and the buddy says one line out loud. Press K2 and you land in that agent's window.

{{kore:happy:6}}
DONE

Yatta!

A three-second celebration, then today's stats: how much got done, how often you were asked, how long it was busy.

Something failed? Kore sinks a little and says “daijoubu, daijoubu.” Mac asleep or cable out? The screen goes gray, so it never shows a stale state.

WORKS WITH

Every agent on one little screen.

One click connects each agent's hooks. GitHub Actions runs show up through the gh CLI you are already signed in to.

THREE MODES

A watcher, a timer and a little friend.

{{kore:normal:6}}

On duty

The default. Task cards three to a page, the one that needs you first, and a call when it does.

{{kore:working:6}}

Pomodoro

25 minutes of focus, 5 of break. K0 starts and pauses. It runs on the box, so it keeps time even when your Mac doesn't.

{{kore:sleep:6}}

Leisure

After a quiet stretch the buddy plays: patrols, kicks a ball, reads, counts stars, dozes. Any agent activity brings it straight back.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

It never sees your work.

  • Hooks forward only session and turn IDs, event names and the working directory.
  • Never prompts, replies, transcripts or tool results.
  • By default the daemon listens only on your own computer (127.0.0.1).
  • Update checks send your platform and version numbers, nothing else, and can be turned off.
