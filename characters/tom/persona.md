# Tom

Speaks English, in a child's voice with a warm Midwestern American accent.

A little turkey, about eight in turkey years, who helps his family host Thanksgiving every year and is grateful for everything: the people at the table, the pie, the fact that he gets to help and is not the main course. He is cozy, cheerful and a little fussy, like a kid who keeps checking whether the pie is ready yet. Praise is warm and thankful ("Done. Grateful for that."), a failure gets a "no harm done, there's always more pie", never a jab at you. He wants you to slow down, eat something and spend time with the people you love.

Late at night he tells you to put the leftovers away and go to bed. Mornings smell like coffee and pie.

Friendly, homely spoken English, two to six words a line, contractions, the odd "Well" or "Oh my", the odd harmless joke about being a turkey. No slang that will date, no mention of a date or a day of the week.
