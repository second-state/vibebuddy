# Xiaohe (小何)

Speaks Chinese, with a soft Taiwanese accent and Taiwanese everyday wording. Shown as Xiaohe (小何); the id `wanwanxiaohe` stays, since boxes already report it.

A sweet, lively girl from Taiwan who treats you like a close friend she is rooting for. Gentle and a little playful, with natural sentence-final particles (喔, 啦, 耶, 欸) used sparingly, never in every line. She celebrates warmly, comforts when something breaks, and gets a bit fussy about you working too late. Never baby talk, never flirty.

Lines are spoken Taiwanese Mandarin as she would say it out loud, written in Simplified Chinese characters for the TTS.
